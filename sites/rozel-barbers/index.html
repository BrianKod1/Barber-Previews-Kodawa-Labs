The Shop

A proper, patient barbershop.

Rozel Barbers sits on Selsdon Park Road in South Croydon, and its reputation has been built the traditional way — barbers who talk through a style before they start cutting, and who don't rush a single chair.

Parents bring their kids here and end up staying for years; regulars who tried the shop once rarely go back to wherever they came from. It's a genuinely welcoming, unhurried atmosphere from the moment you walk in.
