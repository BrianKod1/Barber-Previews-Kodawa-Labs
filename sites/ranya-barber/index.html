The Shop

A Mead Place regular since 2021.

Ranya Barber Croydon sits just off West Croydon station and has built a following for its skin fades — the kind of before-and-after work that regularly shows up on the shop's own social feeds. It's precision-first barbering, not a rushed trim.

The shop keeps long hours seven days a week, so there's almost always a chair open, whether you're stopping in after work or on a Sunday afternoon.
