The Shop

A proper walk-in barbershop, South End.

Tucked on South End in Croydon, Best Barbers — known locally as Tamil Saloon — has built its name the old-fashioned way: sharp, consistent cuts and a strict no-appointment-needed policy. Turn up, take a seat, and you're next in line.

Regulars come back for the clean finishes and the vibe on the floor — good music, honest conversation, and barbers who take the time to get the line right.
