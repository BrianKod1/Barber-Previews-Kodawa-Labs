The Story

From dolls' hair to a chair of her own.

Jacobs started cutting hair at five years old — dolls first, then family members and friends brave enough to sit still. What began as a habit became a trade: she trained and certified through the Ontario College of Trades and the Ministry of Training, Colleges, and Universities.

Today, that same care shapes every appointment at the shop — a small, personal barbering practice built one loyal client at a time.

Still cutting. Still listening.
2016

Jacobs opens her first shop on The Queensway, and builds a loyal following over the next several years.

2020

Pandemic restrictions pause hair styling operations shop-wide, and the Queensway location closes for good the following year.

2022

Jacobs reopens at 170 North Queen Street, welcoming old regulars back and new faces in for the first time.

Today

Still at North Queen Street, still booked by word of mouth — a shop built on showing up, appointment after appointment.
