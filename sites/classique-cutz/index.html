Our Story

Built chair by chair, guest by guest.

Classique Cutz started as a barbershop over six years ago and has grown into a name the Kitchener community trusts. What began as a handful of chairs became a full barbershop & salon, run by partners who've put in the time to earn their reputation one haircut at a time.

Every guest gets a boutique-level experience — high-end products, experienced stylists and barbers, and a level of care that treats each visit as personal, not routine.
