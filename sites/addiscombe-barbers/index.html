The Shop

A proper local, near the tram stop.

Addiscombe Barbers sits on Lower Addiscombe Road, a couple of minutes from Addiscombe tram stop, and has built its name the way a good local shop should — steady regulars, patient barbers, and fair prices that don't creep up every year.

Families bring their kids here, seniors get a reduced rate, and everyone gets the same attention to detail on their fade or beard trim. No booking app required — just walk in.
