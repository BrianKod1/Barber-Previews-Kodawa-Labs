The Shop

A South End regular since 2021.

A1 Barbers has been cutting hair on South End since 2021, building a name for sharp fades, meticulous grooming, and a genuinely welcoming floor. It's the kind of shop where families bring their kids and know they'll get a patient, careful haircut every time.

The shop keeps things simple: clean facilities, attentive barbers, and no appointment required — you show up, you get seen. Regulars say the quality more than justifies the visit.
