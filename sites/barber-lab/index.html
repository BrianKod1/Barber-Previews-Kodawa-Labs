The Shop

Grooming, formulated simply.

Barber Lab sits on Bayly Street West in Ajax, drawing regulars from across Durham Region — Pickering, Whitby, and Oshawa included — with a straightforward promise: skilled fades and beard work at a fair, published price.

It's a walk-in shop through and through. No booking app, no waitlist text — just steady hands, hot towel shaves, and haircuts tailored for kids, seniors, and everyone in between.
