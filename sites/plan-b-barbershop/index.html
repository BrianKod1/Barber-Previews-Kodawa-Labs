The Shop

Old-school barbering, done right.

Plan B Barbershop, led by owner Mr. Milton, keeps the classic barbershop experience alive on West Main Street — black-and-white checkerboard tile, red vinyl chairs, and vintage decor that make every visit feel like a step back to a simpler time.

The shop serves men, women, and kids with everything from low taper fades to precise edge-ups, hot towel shaves, and intricate line designs — with a floor that feels less like a business and more like the neighborhood's living room.
