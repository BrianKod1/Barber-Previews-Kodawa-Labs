The Shop

Precision, not approximation.

Gentlemen's Cut Barber Shop sits in Glenridge Plaza on Lincoln Road in Waterloo — enter from the rear of the building off Larch Street. The shop's reputation rests on meticulous attention to detail, with barbers like Mo known for taking a reference photo and delivering an exact match.

Regulars describe the atmosphere as comfortable and unrushed, with quality products used throughout, and pricing that reviewers consistently call worth every dollar for the level of detail involved.
