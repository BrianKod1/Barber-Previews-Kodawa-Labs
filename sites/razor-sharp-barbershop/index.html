The Shop

La Marque's Neighborhood Barbers

Razor Sharp Barbershop sits on 1st Street in La Marque, Texas, and has built its name on clean, precise cuts — from tight fades to detailed line-ups. It's a shop the whole town knows, and gift cards are even sold through Giftly for locals looking to share the experience.

The shop describes itself as a social, family-friendly environment welcoming to kids, adults, and elders alike, rooted in Christian values. Cuts are available by appointment or walk-in, whichever works for your schedule.
