The Shop

League City's go-to for fresh cuts.

Scissorhands Barbershop sits on Gulf Freeway in League City, run by owner Dedric and a team that includes Tyrone, Lacey, and Kay. The shop specializes in razor cuts, fades, custom designs, and hot towel shaves, with staff trained to work with kids and clients with sensory sensitivities alike.

Same-day appointments and walk-ins are available every day of the week, so the shop works around your schedule rather than the other way around.
