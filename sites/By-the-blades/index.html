The Shop

Ajax's Blade Of Choice

By The Blade Barbers has been on Bayly Street West since 2017, building a reputation across Durham Region for bald fades, straight razor beard lineups, and hot towel shaves that reviewers consistently call worth the wait.

The shop is wheelchair accessible with a custom lift chair, takes both walk-ins and online bookings, and prides itself on barbers who take the time to talk through a style rather than rush through it.
