The Shop

A proper neighbourhood barbers.

Gujarati Barber Hair Salon sits on London Road in Thornton Heath, right in the middle of one of South London's liveliest, most multicultural high streets. It's built a reputation as a genuinely welcoming spot — friendly staff, patient with kids, and prices that stay fair.

The atmosphere gets described as relaxed and chilled rather than rushed, which is part of why regulars keep coming back. Walk-ins are always welcome, though booking ahead is smart if you're going at a busy time.
