The Shop

A dose of distinguished lifestyle.

Distinguished Gents sits on Lake Shore Boulevard West in Etobicoke, built around a nostalgic Italian barbershop atmosphere — think wine barrels and gas-pipe fixtures instead of the usual sterile grooming lounge. It's old-school in feel, sharp in execution.

Razor fades, straight-razor shaves, precise beard work, and even hair-tattoo artistry are all on offer, with regulars specifically requesting Frankie for his attention to detail and unhurried, personal approach.
