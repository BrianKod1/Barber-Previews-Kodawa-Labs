The Shop

Owner Adnan's neighbourhood spot.

Hair and Beard sits on Harwood Avenue South in downtown Ajax, where owner Adnan has built a loyal following on precise cutting techniques and personalized styling — the kind of attention that makes regulars out of first-timers.

The shop keeps things simple: skilled work, reasonable prices, and a genuinely cozy atmosphere that's comfortable for kids and adults alike. Walk-ins are welcome, though booking ahead is smart during peak hours.
