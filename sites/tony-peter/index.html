The Shop

A proper gent's barbers since day one.

Tony & Peter has been a Frith Road fixture for decades, and a recent refit gave it an industrial, old-school-Americana look without losing what made it a neighbourhood institution in the first place.

Regulars have followed the same barber, Joe, for twenty years and counting — the kind of loyalty that only comes from consistently sharp cuts and a shop that treats every visit like a conversation, not a transaction.
